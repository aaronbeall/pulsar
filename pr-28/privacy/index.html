Privacy Policy

Last updated November 10, 2026

The short version

  • Your workouts, routines and settings stay on your device. We never receive them.
  • You don't need an account. If you sign in (only for Pulsar AI), we keep your name, email, photo, sign-in sessions, how much Pulsar AI you've used, and your subscription status.
  • We don't keep what you type to the AI, and we don't use ads, analytics or tracking.
  • Delete your account in Settings at any time and that server data is gone.

This policy covers the Pulsar Workouts app at pulsarworkouts.app/app, this website, and the Pulsar server that supports them. Pulsar Workouts is made by Aaron Beall, doing business as Meta Modern Monkey ("we", "us"). Questions go to support@pulsarworkouts.app.

Data that stays on your device

Everything you create in the app (routines, workouts, sets, streaks, settings, AI chat history and plan history) is stored in your browser's local storage on your device. It is not sent to us, with or without an account. Because it lives only there, clearing your browser's site data or losing your device deletes it. Use Settings > Backup & Restore to keep a copy.

If you add your own OpenAI API key, it is stored on your device too and sent only to OpenAI.

Data we keep if you sign in

An account is optional and only needed for Pulsar AI. When you sign in with Google, our server stores:

WhatWhy
Your name, email address and profile photo from Google, and the sign-in tokens Google issuesTo identify your account and show who's signed in
Sign-in sessions, with the IP address and browser they started fromTo keep you signed in and protect the account
A record of each Pulsar AI use: whether it was a plan or an edit, when, the AI model, the number of tokens and the costTo count your allowance and keep costs in check
Your subscription's Stripe customer and subscription IDs, status, plan and renewal dateTo know whether you have Pulsar AI

We never store your workouts, routines, or the text you send to the AI. We use your email only for your account and, if needed, to contact you about it. We don't send marketing email.

AI features

AI is optional and works in one of two ways:

  • Pulsar AI (signed in): your request goes through our server to OpenAI, and the reply comes back the same way. Our server passes it along without saving it.
  • Your own OpenAI key: your browser sends the request straight to OpenAI. We're not involved.

Either way, OpenAI receives only what the feature needs:

  • Building a routine: your answers to the setup questions.
  • Routine chat: your message, recent messages in that chat, and that routine's name, description and schedule.

Don't include anything in a chat message you wouldn't want OpenAI to process. OpenAI handles this data under its own privacy policy. Nothing is sent unless you use an AI feature.

Payments

Pulsar AI subscriptions are sold through Stripe, which acts as the merchant of record. Stripe collects your payment details, billing address and tax information and sends your receipts, under the Stripe Privacy Policy. We never see your card number. We receive only what's listed above about your subscription.

Cookies and local storage

If you sign in, we set one cookie to keep you signed in. The app also uses your browser's local storage and offline cache to save your data and work without a connection. There are no advertising, analytics or tracking cookies.

How long we keep it

  • Account, AI usage and subscription records: until you delete your account.
  • Sign-in sessions: they expire 90 days after you last used the app, or when you sign out.
  • Server request logs at Cloudflare: a few days.
  • Stripe keeps payment records for as long as tax and financial laws require, even after you delete your Pulsar account.

Your choices and rights

  • Delete your account in Settings > Account. This removes your account, sessions, AI usage and subscription records from our server right away. Cancel an active subscription first, under Manage subscription.
  • Delete your workout data with Settings > Delete All Data, or by clearing the site's data in your browser.
  • Export your workout data with Settings > Backup & Restore.
  • Ask for a copy or a correction of what we hold about you by emailing support@pulsarworkouts.app.

Depending on where you live (for example the EU, UK or California), you may have further rights, such as to object to processing or to complain to your local data protection authority. We'll honor them. We process account data to provide the service you asked for, and payment data to meet our tax and legal obligations.

Security

Traffic is encrypted with HTTPS, sign-in uses Google, and secrets such as API keys are kept out of the app's code. No system is perfectly secure, so keep your device and Google account protected, and back up data you care about.

Children

Pulsar Workouts isn't meant for children under 13, and you must be at least 13 (or older where your country requires it) to create an account. If you think a child has given us personal data, email us and we'll delete it.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top. If a change affects how we use data you've already given us, we'll tell you in the app before it takes effect.
